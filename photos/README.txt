PHOTOS FOLDER

profile.jpg              Your headshot (portrait shape works best, about 4:5).
hobbies/                 Hobby gallery photos, e.g. hobbies/baking-1.jpg

After adding hobby photos, list their file names in the content block
at the top of index.html, e.g.
  { label: "Baking", photos: ["photos/hobbies/baking-1.jpg", "photos/hobbies/baking-2.jpg"] },

Keep each photo under ~500 KB (export at ~1600px wide) so the site stays fast.
